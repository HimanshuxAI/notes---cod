# 🗓️ 10-Week Quant Intern Prep Schedule (Oct 22 – Dec 31, 2026)

**Goal:** Be interview-ready for quant trading/research internships by year-end.  
**Cadence:** 2–3 hours/day on weekdays, 4–6 hours on weekends. Sundays = review + catch-up.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundations (Weeks 1–3): Oct 22 – Nov 11

| Week | Dates | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|------|-------|-------|------------------|
| **W1** | Oct 22–28 | Probability fundamentals | Bayes, conditional probability, expectation, variance; 20+ probability puzzles |
| **W2** | Oct 29–Nov 4 | Statistics + distributions | Normal, Poisson, binomial, exponential; CLT; hypothesis testing basics |
| **W3** | Nov 5–11 | Linear algebra + calculus | Matrix ops, eigenvalues, derivatives, integrals, Taylor series; optimization intuition |

**Weekly rhythm:** Mon–Thu learn + practice problems; Fri–Sat mixed review + puzzles; Sun weekly review.

**Exit criteria (end of W3):** Can solve a Bayes/probability puzzle in <5 min; comfortable with expectation/variance; can do matrix multiplication by hand.

---

## Phase 2 — Coding + Markets (Weeks 4–6): Nov 12 – Dec 2

| Week | Dates | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|------|-------|-------|------------------|
| **W4** | Nov 12–18 | Python for quant | NumPy/pandas vectorization, OOP, datetime, file I/O; implement Black-Scholes from scratch |
| **W5** | Nov 19–25 | C++ for quant | Memory, pointers, STL (vector/map/unordered_map), basic OOP; LeetCode easy/medium |
| **W6** | Nov 26–Dec 2 | Options & derivatives + puzzles | Calls/puts, put-call parity, Greeks (intro), binomial tree; 50+ puzzles solved |

**Weekly rhythm:** Mon–Wed coding; Thu–Fri markets; Sat mixed puzzles + coding; Sun review.

**Exit criteria (end of W6):** Black-Scholes implemented in Python and C++ without looking; can explain put-call parity; solved 50+ quant puzzles.

---

## Phase 3 — Mock Interviews (Weeks 7–8): Dec 3 – Dec 16

| Week | Dates | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|------|-------|-------|------------------|
| **W7** | Dec 3–9 | Mock interview Q&A | Past quant questions (Jane Street, Optiver, Citadel, Hudson River); timed practice |
| **W8** | Dec 10–16 | Weak-spot drilling + full mocks | Re-do missed problems; 2–3 full mock interviews (self-recorded or with a friend) |

**Weekly rhythm:** Daily timed problem sets; every other day a full mock interview; review every wrong answer deeply.

**Exit criteria (end of W8):** Can communicate a clear solution aloud while coding; consistent on easy/medium DSA; hit rate >70% on probability puzzles under time pressure.

---

## Phase 4 — Apply + Close (Weeks 9–10): Dec 17 – Dec 31

| Week | Dates | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|------|-------|-------|------------------|
| **W9** | Dec 17–23 | Applications | Apply to 10+ quant internships; tailor résumé; cover letters where needed |
| **W10** | Dec 24–31 | Final rounds + follow-up | Interview follow-ups, thank-you emails, offer comparison, negotiation prep |

**Daily:** 1–2 applications + 1 hour interview prep; track everything in [`06_Tracker/applications.csv`](../06_Tracker/applications.csv).

**Success metric:** ≥1 first-round interview by Dec 31 (ideally more), with a clear plan to convert in Jan.
