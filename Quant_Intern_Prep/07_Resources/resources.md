# 📚 Quant Intern Prep Resources

**Priority order:** Books → free sites → paid courses. You do NOT need to buy everything.

---

## 🥇 Books (must-read)

| Book | Best for | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Heard on the Street** — Timothy Falcone | Probability puzzles, quant interview classics | The canonical quant interview book. Work through the quantitative reasoning chapter. |
| **A Practical Guide to Quant Finance Interviews** — Xinfeng Zhou | Probability + stats + SHEETS (mental math) | The "SHEETS" section is gold for rapid calculation drills. |
| **Frequently Asked Questions in Quantitative Finance** — Paul Wilmott | Options, derivatives, math depth | Good for markets Q&A and theory. |
| **Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives** — John Hull | Deep derivatives knowledge | Only if targeting research roles; skim chapters on options & Greeks. |

**Tip:** You can find older editions of Falcone and Zhou cheap/used. Focus on the problem-solving, not collecting books.
