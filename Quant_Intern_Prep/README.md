## 🗓️ How to Use This Prep Track

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **Phase 1** | Weeks 1–3 (Oct 22 – Nov 11) | Core concepts: probability, stats, linear algebra, calculus basics |
| **Phase 2** | Weeks 4–6 (Nov 12 – Dec 2) | Coding (Python/C++), DSA, options & derivatives, puzzles |
| **Phase 3** | Weeks 7–8 (Dec 3 – Dec 16) | Mock interviews, past quant questions, weak-spot drilling |
| **Phase 4** | Weeks 9–10 (Dec 17 – Dec 31) | Applications, final interview rounds, offer negotiation |

1. Start at [`00_Timeline/schedule.md`](./00_Timeline/schedule.md) and follow the week-by-week plan.
2. Work through each concept folder, writing code by hand (no IDE crutches) for at least 30 min/day.
3. Track every application and interview in [`06_Tracker/`](./06_Tracker/).
4. Do a weekly review every Sunday — adjust the plan based on what's sticking.

## 📚 Top Target Roles (examples)

- Quant Trader / Quantitative Researcher intern — hedge funds, prop shops, market makers
- Trading Strategist, Research Intern, Developer Intern at quant firms
